Under the Bridge Ministry serves people in North Brevard who are often overlooked: veterans, individuals experiencing homelessness, and families facing financial hardship.[1][2] The ministry reaches roughly 100 people each week with meals, clothing, hygiene resources, and practical support.[1]

## What they do

Based in Titusville, the ministry runs two kinds of service. Volunteers help prepare and serve meals so each guest is received with dignity, and they sort donated clothing and hygiene supplies for distribution.[1][2] Volunteers also take on small facility projects that keep the ministry running smoothly.[1]

Elevation Church regularly serves alongside the ministry, describing it as "a ministry dedicated to supporting veterans, individuals experiencing homelessness, and families facing financial hardship throughout North Brevard."[1] Church groups have served meals and helped organize clothing and supplies on site at 1012 Main Street and 101 North Washington Avenue in Titusville.[1][2]

A separate report notes that in July 2015 the ministry was selected as the recipient of a $1,500 donation recognizing its work providing food, clothing, and spiritual support to homeless and less fortunate people in the Titusville area.[3]

## Why it connects to our work

Under the Bridge Ministry is listed as a partner in the Brevard Continuum of Care — the same coordinating body Base Impact joined in October 2026.[10] That shared membership is where our paths cross: the CoC brings together service providers, agencies, and advocates working to make homelessness rare, brief, and nonrecurring in Brevard County.[10]

Access to technology is part of that picture. Neighbors looking for housing, benefits, or work increasingly need a device and a connection, and the small organizations serving them need working technology to keep their doors open. Supporting partners like Under the Bridge is a natural extension of Base Impact's mission.

## How to help

- **Volunteer on site** — meal service, clothing sorting, and facility projects are recurring needs.[1][2]
- **Donate** — hygiene items, clothing, and food supplies are always useful.[1][2]
- **Refer neighbors** — share the ministry with people in North Brevard who need practical, judgment-free support.
