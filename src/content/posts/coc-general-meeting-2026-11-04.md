On November 4, 2026, Base Impact joined service providers, agencies, and advocates at the Brevard Continuum of Care (CoC) General Membership Meeting, held at the Center for Collaboration.[10]

## What the CoC does

The Continuum of Care is Brevard County's coordinated system for addressing homelessness. Led by the Brevard Homeless Coalition as the designated lead agency, the CoC brings together service providers, government agencies, health services, faith communities, and business partners to make homelessness rare, brief, and nonrecurring in the county.[10]

General membership meetings are held at least quarterly and are open to any individual or organization committed to ending homelessness or assisting people who are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless in Brevard.[10]

## What happened at this meeting

The meeting opened with community announcements and a look at the year ahead. A schedule of 2027 general membership meetings was shared with attendees: February 3, May 5 (the annual meeting), August 4 (the Agency Resource Fair), and November 3. Base Impact was among the organizations in the room, continuing the involvement that began when we joined the CoC in October 2026.

## Why we show up

Our work sits at the intersection of resource access and technology. People experiencing homelessness need reliable access to information, services, and connectivity; the small organizations serving them need working devices and systems. Sitting in the room with Brevard's homelessness partners keeps our resource directory accurate, surfaces where technology support is most needed, and lets us contribute to decisions about housing and services.

## Looking ahead

The CoC meets again in 2027, and we plan to keep showing up — as a member, a resource, and a partner to the organizations doing the front-line work.
