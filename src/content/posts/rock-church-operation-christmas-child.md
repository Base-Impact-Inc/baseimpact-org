At Rock Church in Titusville, volunteers gathered around long tables to pack gift-filled shoeboxes for Operation Christmas Child. Trays of small gifts, tissue paper, and the distinctive orange-and-green boxes filled the fellowship hall as the 2026 collection season got underway.

## Part of a larger effort

Operation Christmas Child is a project of Samaritan's Purse that delivers shoeboxes to children in need around the world, together with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.[4] The 2026 National Collection Week runs November 16–23, when thousands of drop-off locations open across the country.[5][13]

Local packing events like this one are where the work begins: a box, a label, a few carefully chosen gifts, and a note of encouragement. From there, the boxes travel to churches in more than 100 nations, where children receive them at outreach events.[13]

## Why churches host packing events

The project partners with local churches around the world to deliver the boxes.[13] For a congregation, packing together is a practical way to serve — children, families, and small groups can all take part, and the finished box is something tangible to send.

Rock Church and Base Impact already work together on community projects, including a home rebuild earlier this year. This was another chance to put hands to work for neighbors far away.

## Get involved

- Find a drop-off location near you before November 23.[5]
- Pack a box at home or join a local group packing event.[13]
- Give $10 per box to cover shipping and ministry costs.[5]
