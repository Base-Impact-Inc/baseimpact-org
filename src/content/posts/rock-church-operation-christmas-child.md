At Rock Church in Titusville, volunteers gathered around long tables to pack gift-filled shoeboxes for Operation Christmas Child. Trays of small gifts, tissue paper, and the distinctive orange-and-green boxes filled the fellowship hall as the 2026 collection season got underway.

It is a simple scene — a box, a label, a few carefully chosen items — but it connects a local church to children halfway around the world.

## What the boxes do

Operation Christmas Child is a project of Samaritan's Purse that delivers gift-filled shoeboxes to children in need, together with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.[4] The boxes travel to churches in more than 100 nations, where children receive them at outreach events.[13]

This year's **National Collection Week runs November 16–23, 2026**, when more than 4,800 drop-off locations open across the United States.[4][5] In Florida, the Samaritan's Purse regional office serves the state and Puerto Rico out of Orlando and can connect volunteers with a nearby drop-off site.[6]

## Why the local packing event matters

A packing event turns a global idea into something a neighbor can actually do. Children, families, and small groups can all take part. The finished box is tangible — something a person can hold, label, and pray over before it leaves town.

Rock Church and Base Impact already work together on community projects, including a home rebuild earlier this year. This was another chance to put hands to work, this time for children most of us will never meet.

For Base Impact, that is the point. Our community is full of people who want to help but need a clear, realistic way to start. We help by making opportunities visible, explaining the practical details, and connecting neighbors with groups already doing the work. A shoebox drive is accessible: no specialized skill required, just care and a few supplies.

## How to pack a box

Packing is straightforward:[5][6]

- Choose one "wow" item, such as a doll, stuffed animal, or toy car
- Add personal care items, school supplies, and craft items
- Include a suggested **$10 donation** to cover shipping, processing, and discipleship materials
- Add a "Follow Your Box" label if you want to track where it goes
- Leave out food, candy, liquids, medication, money, breakable items, and knives or razors

## Other ways to help

During collection week, volunteers welcome people dropping off boxes, pray with them, fill cartons, and load trucks. Roughly 98,000 people serve in these roles nationwide each year.[6] It is a short, high-energy commitment that happens in a single week but reaches children in more than 100 countries.[4][6]

To take part:

- Find a nearby drop-off location through the Samaritan's Purse locator before **November 23**.[5]
- Pack a box at home or join a local group packing event.[13]
- Build a box online or mail one to a permanent ministry center if you cannot reach a drop-off site.[5]
- Give $10 per box to cover shipping and ministry costs.[5][6]
