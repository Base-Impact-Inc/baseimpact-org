On October 27, 2026, Base Impact helped an 80+ year-old neighbor move into her new home. This is the kind of hands-on community support that goes beyond our technology mission — but it's exactly the kind of help our neighbors need.

## The situation

An elderly neighbor in Brevard County was transitioning into a new home. At 80+ years old, she needed physical help with the move — packing, lifting, transporting, and setting up her new space. She didn't have family nearby to assist.

## What we did

Our volunteers showed up to help with every step of the move:
- **Packing and loading** — Carefully packing her belongings and loading them for transport
- **Transportation** — Moving her furniture and boxes to the new home
- **Unpacking and setup** — Helping her settle into her new space, arranging furniture, and making sure she was comfortable
- **Safety check** — Ensuring the new home was safe and accessible for her

## Why this matters

At Base Impact, we believe that technology help and community care go hand in hand. Many of the neighbors we serve with devices and digital skills also face housing instability, isolation, and the challenges of aging. When we see a neighbor who needs help — whether it's setting up a laptop or moving into a new home — we show up.

## Technology for seniors

This experience also reinforced why our technology access work matters. Many seniors in our community are isolated and struggle with basic digital tasks. Helping this neighbor move reminded us that the same people who need physical help often also need help with:
- Setting up phones and tablets to stay connected with family
- Learning to use video calling to reduce isolation
- Accessing online services for healthcare and benefits
- Finding community resources through our directory
