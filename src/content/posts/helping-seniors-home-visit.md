Some of the most useful help happens at a kitchen table, not in a classroom. Base Impact volunteers visited a senior neighbor at home to work through technology questions face to face — the kind of patient, one-on-one support that a phone call or a website can't replace.

## Why home visits matter

Older neighbors often have the most to gain from technology and the most trouble getting to it. Transportation, mobility, or simply not knowing where to start can keep someone from getting help. In Titusville, [Helping Seniors of Brevard](https://helpingseniorsofbrevard.info/) is one of the local organizations focused on senior resources, and the [Brevard Resource Guide](https://www.titusville.com/DocumentCenter/View/6280/Brevard-Resource-Guide-2026) lists it as a senior-services contact.[21]

Base Impact's role is complementary: when a neighbor needs a device set up, an account recovered, or a safe way to handle email and the internet, we can come to them.

## What we help with

- **Device setup** — getting a phone, tablet, or computer working and connected
- **Accounts and passwords** — recovering access and setting up secure, memorable credentials
- **Safe internet use** — spotting scam messages, phishing, and suspicious calls
- **Practical tasks** — email, video calls with family, online forms, and benefits portals
- **A patient explanation** — taking the time to go through it together, at the neighbor's pace

## The bigger picture

Technology access is a quality-of-life issue for seniors. It's how they stay in touch with family, manage appointments, and reach services. A single home visit can remove a barrier that has been in place for years.
