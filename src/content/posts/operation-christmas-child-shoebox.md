Operation Christmas Child is a project of Samaritan's Purse that sends gift-filled shoeboxes to children in need around the world, together with the message of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.[4] Each box is packed with small toys, school supplies, and personal care items.

## National Collection Week 2026

The collection season runs during **National Collection Week, November 16–23, 2026**, when more than 4,800 drop-off locations open across the United States.[4][5] In Florida, the Samaritan's Purse regional office serves the state and Puerto Rico out of Orlando and can connect volunteers with a nearby drop-off site.[6]

Packing a box is straightforward: choose one "wow" item, add personal care, school, and craft items, and include a suggested $10 donation to cover shipping, processing, and discipleship materials.[5][6] Boxes can be tracked with a "Follow Your Box" label. Food, candy, liquids, medication, money, breakable items, and knives or razors should be left out.[6]

## How volunteers help

During collection week, volunteers welcome people dropping off boxes, pray with them, fill cartons, and load trucks. Roughly 98,000 people serve in these roles nationwide each year.[6] It's a short, high-energy commitment that happens in a single week but reaches children in more than 100 countries.[4][6]

## Why it matters locally

For Brevard families, a shoebox drive is an accessible way to serve together — churches, schools, and small groups can pack boxes as an office, family, or community project. Base Impact sees the same need our neighbors do: practical generosity, delivered with care, to people who rarely get a choice in what they receive.

## How to take part

- Find a nearby drop-off location through the Samaritan's Purse locator before November 23.[5]
- Build a box online or mail one to a permanent ministry center if you can't reach a drop-off site.[5]
- Give $10 per box to cover shipping and ministry costs.[5][6]
