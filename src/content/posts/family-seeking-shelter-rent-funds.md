Base Impact is currently walking alongside a family in Brevard County who is facing housing instability. We're connecting them with shelter resources, rental assistance programs, and the information they need to find stable housing.

> **Privacy note:** This story is shared with the family's awareness. Details are kept general to protect their privacy. Full publication will wait for their consent.

## The situation

A family reached out to Base Impact after falling behind on rent and facing the possibility of losing their housing. With limited income and few local connections, navigating the patchwork of shelter programs, rental assistance, and emergency housing felt overwhelming. They needed someone to help them find the right doors — and walk through them.

## What we're doing

- **Resource navigation** — Walked the family through available shelter programs, emergency housing, and rental assistance options in Brevard, Volusia, and Orange counties
- **Application support** — Helped complete and submit applications for rental assistance and emergency housing programs
- **Information and referrals** — Connected them with local organizations that provide direct rent relief and shelter placement
- **Follow-up** — Staying in touch to track application status and adjust the plan as needed
- **Emotional support** — Being a consistent, caring presence during a stressful time

## Current status

This is an ongoing situation. The family is actively working through applications and has been connected with multiple local resources. We're monitoring progress and adjusting support as their needs change.

## Why housing stability matters

A family losing housing doesn't just lose a roof — it disrupts children's schooling, parents' employment, health routines, and community ties. Early intervention and resource connection can prevent a temporary crisis from becoming long-term homelessness. This is exactly the kind of neighbor-to-neighbor support Base Impact exists to provide.
