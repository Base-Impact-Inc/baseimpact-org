At [LifePointe Ministries](https://www.lifepointeministries.org/) in Titusville, neighbors gathered for a free community event on how to recognize scams and protect personal information. The session featured detectives from the Titusville Police Department taking questions after the presentation.

## What the event covered

The flyer for the event — photographed at the gathering — spelled out three practical goals: learn about common scams and fraud tactics, get tips to protect yourself and your loved ones, and ask questions of detectives from the Titusville Police Department. Doors opened at 10 a.m. with refreshments, and the presentation began at 10:30 a.m. on Wednesday, October 21. The flyer named LifePointe Ministries as host.

## Why this matters

Fraud is a daily threat for older adults and anyone managing bills, benefits, or bank accounts online. The [City of Titusville](https://titusville.com/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=241) has warned that scammers have spoofed the police department's non-emergency number to solicit personal information and money. Its guidance is blunt: the police department does not call to ask for personal or banking information, and you should never give either over the phone to an unsolicited caller.[20]

That message is exactly what digital-safety education is for. A scam can cost someone their savings in a single phone call; knowing the warning signs costs nothing.

## Base Impact's role

Our work puts us in touch with neighbors who are online for the first time, setting up email, applying for jobs, or managing benefits. Those are the same moments when scam awareness matters most. Partnering with a church and a police department to put that information within reach is a natural fit for our mission.

## Get involved

Watch for community education events at [LifePointe Ministries](https://www.lifepointeministries.org/) (4220 South Hopkins Avenue, Titusville) and follow the [Titusville Police Department](https://titusville.com/494/Police-Department) alerts. If you're unsure about a call or message, hang up and verify independently.
