No One Hungry is a faith-based nonprofit food pantry ministry in Titusville with a motto of "Reducing Hunger...Giving Hope."[7] What began as one parishioner's sense of calling in 2014 grew into a ministry serving individuals, families, seniors, and people experiencing homelessness across North Brevard.[7][8]

## From one calling to two pantries

The ministry is a partnership of St. Gabriel's Episcopal Church and Bethlehem Missionary Baptist Church in Titusville.[7] It opened its first pantry in 2015, followed by a pantry for seniors, and began home deliveries to seniors that same year.[7] By its first full year it had distributed more than 34,000 pounds of food in North Brevard.[7] Today its Pine Street pantry is open Tuesdays from 10 a.m. to noon at 418 Pine Street in downtown Titusville.[7][9]

No One Hungry incorporated as a Florida faith-based nonprofit and received its 501(c)(3) status in 2019.[8] It is an agency partner of Second Harvest Food Bank and reports assisting more than 7,000 families annually in the Titusville area.[8][9]

## More than food

The ministry's reach goes beyond pantry shelves. Volunteers deliver food to seniors who can't travel, take food and personal items directly to people experiencing homelessness, and distribute socks, insect repellent, clothing, and hygiene supplies alongside meals.[7][9] It has also partnered with Parrish Medical Center's Partners Healing Program to provide temporary food assistance to qualifying patients at discharge.[9]

"That calling grew into a desire to help senior citizens fulfill their basic need for food pantry items during the month."[7]

## How to help

- **Volunteer** — pack or deliver food, or serve as a service provider partner.[7]
- **Donate** — funds, food items, and support services are all welcomed.[7][9]
- **Spread the word** — the pantry is at 418 Pine Street, Titusville, Tuesdays 10 a.m.–noon; call 321-567-4408.[7][9]
