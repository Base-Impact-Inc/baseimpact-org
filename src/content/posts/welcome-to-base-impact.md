Base Impact Inc. was founded to bring technology help where it's needed most. We work alongside local organizations, churches, pantries, and small nonprofits to provide free device setup, security, and ongoing support.

## Our mission

To connect people with the technology they need to thrive — from refurbished phones and tablets to digital skills training and AI-powered tools for small nonprofits.

## What we do

- **Technology setup & support** — Free device setup, security, and ongoing support for 1–5 person community groups.
- **Resource sharing** — Our directory lists local services alongside 211, making it easier for neighbors to find help.
- **Volunteer coordination** — We connect skilled volunteers with organizations that need tech help.

## Get involved

If your organization serves your community and could benefit from technology support, [reach out](/contact). We'd love to hear from you.
