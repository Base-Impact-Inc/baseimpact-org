Base Impact is now a member of the Brevard Homeless Continuum of Care (CoC) coalition. This membership connects us with the network of organizations, agencies, and advocates working together to end homelessness in Brevard County.

## What is the CoC?

The Continuum of Care (CoC) is a community-wide planning body that coordinates housing and services for people experiencing homelessness. It brings together nonprofits, government agencies, faith-based organizations, and community members to:

- **Assess needs** — Understanding the scope and nature of homelessness in the community
- **Coordinate resources** — Aligning housing programs, shelter beds, and support services
- **Apply for funding** — Jointly applying for federal (HUD) and state homelessness funding
- **Set priorities** — Deciding how to allocate resources for maximum impact
- **Track progress** — Measuring outcomes and adjusting strategies

## Why Base Impact joined

Our mission is to connect people with resources and help small nonprofits keep their technology working. Homelessness intersects with both: people experiencing homelessness need access to information, services, and connectivity, and the small organizations serving them need working technology.

By joining the CoC, Base Impact can:
- **Contribute our resource directory** — Sharing our verified local resource data with the coalition
- **Offer technology support** — Helping member organizations with device, internet, and digital-skills needs
- **Stay informed** — Understanding the latest on homelessness trends, funding, and programs in Brevard
- **Collaborate** — Partnering with other organizations on shared goals
- **Advocate** — Representing the technology-access perspective in housing discussions

## What this means for our work

Membership in the CoC strengthens Base Impact's ability to serve neighbors experiencing homelessness. It gives us a seat at the table where decisions are made about housing and services in Brevard County, and it connects us with partners who can amplify our impact.
