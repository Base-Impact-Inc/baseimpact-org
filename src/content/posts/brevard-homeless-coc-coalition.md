Base Impact is now a member of the Brevard Homeless Continuum of Care (CoC) coalition. This membership connects us with the network of organizations, agencies, and advocates working together to end homelessness in Brevard County. Less than a week after joining in October 2026, we were at the table.

## What is the CoC?

The Continuum of Care is Brevard County's coordinated system for addressing homelessness. Led by the Brevard Homeless Coalition as the designated lead agency, it brings together nonprofits, government agencies, faith communities, health services, and business partners to make homelessness **rare, brief, and nonrecurring**.[10]

As a community-wide planning body, the CoC:

- **Assesses needs** — Understanding the scope and nature of homelessness in the community
- **Coordinates resources** — Aligning housing programs, shelter beds, and support services
- **Applies for funding** — Jointly applying for federal (HUD) and state homelessness funding
- **Sets priorities** — Deciding how to allocate resources for maximum impact
- **Tracks progress** — Measuring outcomes and adjusting strategies

General membership meetings are held at least quarterly and are open to any individual or organization committed to ending homelessness or assisting people who are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless in Brevard.[10]

## Why Base Impact joined

Our mission is to connect people with resources and help small nonprofits keep their technology working. Homelessness intersects with both: people experiencing homelessness need access to information, services, and connectivity, and the small organizations serving them need working technology.

By joining the CoC, Base Impact can:

- **Contribute our resource directory** — Sharing our verified local resource data with the coalition
- **Offer technology support** — Helping member organizations with device, internet, and digital-skills needs
- **Stay informed** — Understanding the latest on homelessness trends, funding, and programs in Brevard
- **Collaborate** — Partnering with other organizations on shared goals
- **Advocate** — Representing the technology-access perspective in housing discussions

## At the table: the November 2026 meeting

On November 4, 2026, Base Impact joined service providers, agencies, and advocates at the CoC General Membership Meeting at the Center for Collaboration.[10] The meeting opened with community announcements and a look at the year ahead, including the 2027 general membership calendar:

- **February 3, 2027**
- **May 5, 2027** — annual meeting
- **August 4, 2027** — Agency Resource Fair
- **November 3, 2027**

Sitting in the room with Brevard's homelessness partners keeps our resource directory accurate, surfaces where technology support is most needed, and lets us contribute to decisions about housing and services.

## What this means for our work

Membership in the CoC strengthens Base Impact's ability to serve neighbors experiencing homelessness. It gives us a seat at the table where decisions are made about housing and services in Brevard County, and it connects us with partners who can amplify our impact. We plan to keep showing up — as a member, a resource, and a partner to the organizations doing the front-line work.
