Base Impact is proud to be part of the community response supporting the Willem family of DeBary, Florida. Matthew Willem spent decades being told his severe symptoms were anxiety or laziness — until a colonoscopy revealed Crohn's disease. The delayed diagnosis cost him health, income, and stability. But the West Volusia community rallied around him.

## The story

Matthew Willem, 46, of DeBary, FL, traced his earliest serious symptoms to around 1999. An avid weightlifter in high school, he began experiencing unexplained episodes of severe nausea and vomiting. Doctors frequently attributed his symptoms to anxiety. He was hospitalized in spring 2026, and a colonoscopy revealed signs of Crohn's disease. His gastroenterologist confirmed the diagnosis in July 2026.

The diagnosis brought relief — but also the reality of expensive treatment, insurance complications, and the demands of caring for a family already facing significant medical challenges.

## The family

- **Matthew** — diagnosed with Crohn's disease, navigating treatment with Skyrizi, facing severe fatigue, pain, and unpredictable flares
- **Lisa** — managing her own health challenges, including lingering nerve pain from shingles
- **Afton (12)** — navigating childhood while illness and financial uncertainty affect the household
- **Mark (10)** — nonverbal, autistic (level 3), with ADHD, developmental delays, and other needs requiring close supervision and daily care

The family also helps care for Lisa's adult brother, who has schizophrenia. Their 2007 Hyundai Sonata is essential for medical appointments, treatment, school, and everyday needs.

## How the community responded

The Willems found encouragement from people and organizations throughout West Volusia:

- **Local churches** — First Baptist Church of DeBary, St. Ann's Catholic Church, and DeBary United Methodist Church provided groceries and clothing
- **Blue Line Automotive** — helped the family keep their aging vehicle operating
- **Base Impact** — connected the family with resources and technology support

Matthew wants to return the generosity by volunteering his technology and computer skills. He hopes to help other people, particularly those living with chronic illnesses that aren't immediately apparent.
