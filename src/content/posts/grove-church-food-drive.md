Under a blue-and-white canopy in Titusville, volunteers gathered around folding tables stacked with boxes for a community food drive at The Grove Church. It's the kind of work that happens quietly, without much fanfare — and it's exactly the kind of work that keeps neighbors fed.

## A church with a history of feeding people

The Grove Church has been part of Titusville's food-security network for years. In 2020, meals donated by a local business were distributed through The Grove Church to families with children who couldn't get lunch from school during the pandemic.[14] The church was also instrumental in helping Under the Bridge Ministry get started — one of North Brevard's best-known food and outreach ministries.[11]

The congregation meets at 1450 Harrison Street in Titusville.[12] Its members have also partnered on other community efforts, including the annual "Kicks for Kids" shoe drive for local schoolchildren.[15]

## Why food drives still matter

Food insecurity doesn't make headlines, but it shapes daily life for many families in Brevard County. A box of groceries can free up money for rent, medicine, or a utility bill. Local drives also build something less visible: a network of people who know each other and can be reached when the next need shows up.

That's the thread connecting the food drive, Under the Bridge Ministry, No One Hungry, and the other North Brevard pantries. Different groups, same work — making sure no neighbor goes without.

## How to help

- **Donate food** at a local drive or pantry.
- **Volunteer** to sort, pack, or deliver.
- **Share the need** — many neighbors don't know where to turn, and a single conversation can connect them with help.
