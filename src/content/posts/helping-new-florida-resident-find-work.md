Base Impact helped a new Florida resident find work and get connected — all in a single visit. We assisted her with online job applications, set up her donated computer on-site, and provided hands-on training. This is the kind of comprehensive help that changes lives.

## The situation

A woman had recently moved to Florida and was starting fresh. She needed a job, didn't have a working computer, and wasn't familiar with online job search platforms. She also needed help setting up a computer that had been donated to her but wasn't configured or ready to use.

## What we did

In a single on-site visit, our volunteers:

### 1. Set up her donated computer
- **Unboxed and configured** the donated computer
- **Connected to Wi-Fi** and tested the internet connection
- **Installed essential software** — web browser, email, and job search tools
- **Set up an email account** for professional communication
- **Configured security** — antivirus, firewall, and automatic updates

### 2. Helped with job applications
- **Created profiles** on major job search platforms (Indeed, LinkedIn, etc.)
- **Searched for local jobs** matching her skills and experience
- **Filled out applications** together, explaining each step
- **Uploaded her resume** and cover letter to job portals
- **Set up job alerts** so new opportunities would come to her email

### 3. Provided on-site training
- **Basic computer skills** — How to use the mouse, keyboard, and navigate the desktop
- **Internet basics** — How to search, use bookmarks, and stay safe online
- **Email management** — How to read, reply, and organize emails
- **Job search strategies** — How to track applications and follow up
- **Online safety** — How to recognize scams and protect personal information

## The impact

By the end of the visit, this new Florida resident had:
- A **working computer** with internet access
- A **professional email** account
- **Active job search profiles** on multiple platforms
- **Completed job applications** for local positions
- **The skills and confidence** to continue searching on her own

## Why this matters

Starting over in a new state is hard. Without a working device and digital skills, finding a job becomes nearly impossible. By providing both the hardware and the training in a single visit, we removed multiple barriers at once.
