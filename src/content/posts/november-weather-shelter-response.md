Over the past few days, Base Impact helped alongside volunteers at [LifePointe Ministries](https://www.lifepointeministries.org/) as the Titusville church opened its inclement-weather shelter during a stretch of heavy rain and flooding in Brevard County. This was the shelter in action — the same team we had trained with in September (see [Inclement Weather Shelter Training Day](/blog/lifepointe-cold-weather-shelter)) now serving neighbors through a real weather event.

[News 6 / ClickOrlando reported the opening on November 18, 2026](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/18/titusville-ministry-opens-weather-shelter-as-brevard-county-braces-for-more-rain-flooding/).

## Why the shelter opened

LifePointe opened its doors at 5 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18 and kept the shelter running around the clock until 9 a.m. Friday, November 20. The church usually opens the shelter for extreme cold, but prolonged rain can be just as dangerous for people without a safe, dry place to stay.

Pastor Royce "Scooter" Morrison told News 6 that he saw people huddled under benches and trees on his way to the church, and immediately called his team. Associate pastor Richard Evans had already opened the building the night before so a few people could get out of the rain before the official opening.

## What neighbors received inside

Volunteers set up cots, pillows, and blankets. The shelter offered hot meals, clean clothing, hygiene supplies, showers, and a dry place to sleep. People could bring pets, which were accommodated in a separate facility next door.

The shelter was opened independently of Brevard County government. County officials said they were prepared to open county-run shelters if conditions warranted.

## Where Base Impact fits in

Our role in moments like this is practical: help where hands are needed, and make sure people can connect to longer-term services once the immediate danger passes.

- **Volunteer support** — working alongside LifePointe's team during setup and daily operations
- **Resource navigation** — connecting guests with our [resource directory](/directory) for food, shelter, hygiene, and transportation help
- **Community awareness** — sharing accurate, timely information about where shelter is available
- **Technology help** — keeping devices and information usable for the people and organizations doing the work

## How you can help

If you can volunteer, donate supplies, or simply share accurate shelter information, contact [LifePointe Ministries](https://www.lifepointeministries.org/) or reach out through our [contact page](/contact). On a bad-weather night, a dry place to sleep and a warm meal can change everything.
