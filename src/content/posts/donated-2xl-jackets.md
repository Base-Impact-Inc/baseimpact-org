On November 1, 2026, Base Impact donated 2XL jackets to neighbors in need across Brevard County. As temperatures began to drop, we recognized that many of our neighbors — especially those experiencing homelessness or living without reliable heating — needed warm clothing.

## Why jackets matter

A warm jacket isn't just about comfort. For someone sleeping outdoors or in an unheated space, it can be a matter of health and safety. Many of our neighbors who come to us for technology help also face basic survival challenges like staying warm.

## What we did

We collected and distributed 2XL jackets to individuals who needed them most. Each jacket was clean, in good condition, and ready to wear. We worked with local partners to identify recipients and make sure the jackets reached the people who needed them.

## Technology and community

At Base Impact, we believe that helping neighbors means addressing all their needs — not just technology. While our core mission is connecting people with devices, internet, and digital skills, we also step up when the community has immediate needs like warm clothing, food, or shelter referrals.
