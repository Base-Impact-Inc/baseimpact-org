Base Impact stepped in to help a neighbor who was at risk of losing their internet service over an unpaid bill. For many families, internet isn't a luxury — it's how they apply for jobs, attend school, access telehealth, and stay in touch with loved ones.

## The situation

A neighbor in Brevard County reached out after falling behind on their internet bill. Without service, they faced losing access to online job applications, school portals for their children, and the ability to manage benefits and appointments. The stress of potentially losing connectivity — on top of everything else — was overwhelming.

## What we did

- **Assessed the need** — Reviewed the bill, the household's situation, and available options
- **Connected them with resources** — Identified local assistance programs and community partners who could help with the balance
- **Provided direct support** — Base Impact contributed toward the bill so service could be restored
- **Set up for success** — Helped the neighbor explore lower-cost plans and assistance programs for ongoing affordability
- **Followed up** — Checked back to make sure service stayed active

## The impact

With internet restored, this neighbor can now:
- **Apply for jobs** and complete online applications
- **Access telehealth** appointments and medical portals
- **Support their children's** schoolwork and remote learning
- **Manage benefits** and appointments online
- **Stay connected** with family and community resources

## Why internet access matters

The digital divide isn't just about devices — it's about the connection itself. When a family loses internet, they lose access to nearly every system that runs modern life. Utility assistance for connectivity is one of the most direct ways we can help a neighbor stay afloat.
