Base Impact joined volunteers at [LifePointe Ministries](https://www.lifepointeministries.org/) in Titusville to help prepare cots and learn how the church's cold-weather shelter operates. The session covered setup, hospitality, and what it takes to open a shelter quickly when temperatures drop.

## Who runs the shelter

LifePointe Ministries, at 4220 South Hopkins Avenue in Titusville, operates the only inclement-weather shelter between Titusville and Port Orange.[16] The ministry and the [Titusville Civic Center](https://titusvilleciviccenter.com/lifepointe-ministries) provide warm food, a change of clothing, blankets, showers, and hygiene items — needs that go well beyond a place to sleep.[16]

The work is volunteer-run from top to bottom. Pastor Royce "Scooter" Morrison has said the outreach is "100% volunteer-based" and that even the pastor draws no pay for it.[19] During one recent cold spell, the shelter was open 12 of 26 days and all 60-plus beds were full.[19]

## CREW 29

The outreach arm of LifePointe is called C.R.E.W. 29 — a weekly gathering of volunteers who run showers, hand out clothing from the church warehouse, provide a warm meal, and help neighbors with practical and spiritual needs.[17] The name is on the green lanyards volunteers wear. LifePointe describes the team as "a large group effort" drawing support from area churches.[22]

## What the training covered

- **Cot setup** — moving cots into place and arranging a hall for overnight guests
- **Intake and welcome** — receiving guests respectfully and assigning sleeping space
- **Hospitality** — meals, showers, clean clothing, and a calm, dignified environment
- **Team coordination** — how shifts, volunteers, and donations come together on short notice

## Why Base Impact was there

Shelter work and technology access overlap more than people expect. Guests need to reach services, benefits, and family; the organizations running shelters need working devices, internet, and reliable information to keep operations moving. Training alongside LifePointe helps us understand where our directory and tech support can actually help.
